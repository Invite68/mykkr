📜 Historical Timeline
Kurukshetra is named after King Kuru. The Kurukshetra War of the Mahabharata took place here. The Bhagavad Gita was delivered by Lord Krishna to Arjuna at Jyotisar before the battle.
  • c.1200–900 BCEKuru Kingdom — first state-level society in Indian subcontinent. Vedas compiled here.
  • 4th century BCEMauryan Empire. Centre of Buddhism and Hinduism.
  • 590–647 CEPeak under King Harsha. Chinese scholar Xuanzang visited Thanesar.
  • 1014 CESacked by Mahmud of Ghazni.
  • 1526 CEMughal Empire. Under Akbar — spiritual centre for Hindus, Sikhs, Muslims.
  • 1805 CEBritish took Kurukshetra after Second Anglo-Maratha War.
  • 1947–presentMajor restoration of sacred sites. Most visited pilgrimage city in Haryana.
