📜 Historical Timeline
Kurukshetra is named after King Kuru, who chose the banks of the Saraswati River to cultivate spirituality. The Kurukshetra War of the Mahabharata took place here. The Bhagavad Gita was delivered by Lord Krishna to Arjuna at Jyotisar before the battle.
  • c.1200–900 BCEKuru Kingdom — first state-level society in Indian subcontinent. Vedas compiled here.
  • 4th century BCEConquered by Mauryan Empire. Centre of Buddhism and Hinduism.
  • 590–647 CEPeak under King Harsha. Chinese scholar Xuanzang visited Thanesar.
  • 1014 CESacked by Mahmud of Ghazni.
  • 1526 CEMughal Empire after Babur. Under Akbar — spiritual centre for Hindus, Sikhs, Muslims.
  • 1805 CEBritish took Kurukshetra after Second Anglo-Maratha War.
  • 1947–presentMajor restoration of sacred sites. Most visited pilgrimage city in Haryana.
🌊
Brahma Sarovar
Asia's largest man-made sacred tank. Lakhs of pilgrims bathe during Somavati Amavasya and solar eclipses. Hindu genealogy registers kept here.
Sacred Site
🙏
Jyotisar — Gita Birthplace
The exact spot under a sacred peepul tree where Lord Krishna delivered the Bhagavad Gita to Arjuna. Ancient tree still stands. Bronze chariot marks the site.
Pilgrimage
⚔️
Kurukshetra Panorama
360-degree mural depicting the Mahabharata war combined with a science centre. A must-visit heritage experience in the city.
Museum
🛕
Sthaneshwar Mahadev
One of North India's most ancient Shiva temples. Pandavas prayed here before the Mahabharata war. Active place of worship visited daily.
Temple
🌺
Bhadrakali Temple
Powerful goddess temple. Devotees from across Haryana and Punjab come for blessings, especially during Navratri.
Temple
💧
Sannihit Sarovar
Sacred lake where all seven Saraswatis are believed to converge. Bathing on Amavasya equal to Ashvamedha Yajna.
Sacred Site
🏛️
Sheikh Chilli's Tomb
Mughal-era monument maintained by ASI. Built for Sufi Saint Sheikh Chehli, associated with Prince Dara Shikoh.
Monument
📚
Dharohar Museum
Museum of Haryana's cultural heritage inside KU campus. Showcases traditional Haryanvi culture, arts, and way of life.
Museum

  • Gita JayantiInternational Gita Mahotsav — Shukla Ekadashi of Margashirsha. Celebrates the day Bhagavad Gita was revealed. Massive state-level fair with cultural programmes.
  • Somavati AmavasyaNew moon on Monday — lakhs arrive to bathe in Brahmasarovar. One of Haryana's largest spontaneous gatherings.
  • Solar EclipseDuring every solar eclipse, millions come to bathe in Brahmasarovar. Sometimes 10–20 lakh people in a single day.
  • 48 Kos ParikramaTraditional pilgrimage on foot through all sacred sites in a ~90 km circle around the holy city.
  • NavratriBhadrakali Temple becomes the centre of massive celebrations with devotees from Haryana and Punjab.
  • Kurukshetra FestivalAnnual cultural festival organized by the state government showcasing Haryanvi culture, crafts, and folk arts.
