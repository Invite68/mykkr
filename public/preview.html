🌊
Brahmsarovar
The largest sacred tank in India. Thousands of pilgrims take a holy dip here, especially during solar eclipses when lakhs come from across India.
Sacred Site
🙏
Jyotisar — Birthplace of the Gita
The exact spot under a sacred peepul tree where Lord Krishna delivered the Bhagavad Gita to Arjuna before the Mahabharata war.
Pilgrimage
⚔️
Kurukshetra Panorama
A stunning 360-degree painting depicting the Mahabharata war. A must-visit science and heritage centre in the heart of the city.
Museum
🛕
Sthaneshwar Mahadev
One of the most ancient Shiva temples in North India. The Pandavas are said to have prayed here before the Mahabharata war.
Temple
🌺
Bhadrakali Temple
A powerful goddess temple with deep historical significance. Visitors from across Haryana come for blessings, especially during Navratri.
Temple
🏛️
Sannihit Sarovar
A sacred lake where all holy rivers are believed to converge on the new moon night. An important pilgrimage site in Thanesar city.
Sacred Site
