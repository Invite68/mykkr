Official nameKurukshetra (कुरुक्षेत्र)
Also known asDharmakshetra · Land of the Bhagavad Gita
StateHaryana, India
Named afterKing Kuru — ancestor of Kauravas and Pandavas
Holy city statusDeclared 2017 — meat sale/consumption banned
RailwayKurukshetra Junction (Delhi–Kalka line)
📜 Timeline
  • c.1200–900 BCEKuru Kingdom — Vedas compiled here. First state-level society in India.
  • 590–647 CEPeak under King Harsha. Chinese scholar Xuanzang visited.
  • 1526 CEMughal Empire. Spiritual centre for Hindus, Sikhs, and Muslims.
  • 1947–presentMajor restoration of sacred sites. Most visited pilgrimage city in Haryana.

Privacy Policy
Effective date: 1 November 2025 · My KKR, Kurukshetra, Haryana, India
1. What data we collect
From customers: Name, mobile number (verified via OTP), email address, area/locality in Kurukshetra.
From businesses: Business name, owner name, mobile number, email, business address, category, photos.
From ride/delivery requests: Name, mobile number, pickup location, drop-off location, vehicle type. Your number is hidden from drivers until assignment.
Automatically collected: No cookies. No tracking pixels. No analytics scripts. We collect only what you provide.
2. Why we collect it
Legal basis: Your explicit consent (checkbox at registration).
• To connect customers with local businesses in Kurukshetra
• To verify your identity via OTP
• To process ₹49/month or ₹299/month subscriptions via Razorpay
• To dispatch ride and delivery requests to nearby drivers via Telegram
• To send booking notifications via SMS/WhatsApp
3. Who we share it with
Razorpay — payment processing (PCI-DSS compliant). We never see your card or UPI details.
Firebase (Google) — phone OTP authentication only.
Telegram — ride/delivery job broadcast to registered drivers. Driver details shared with you upon assignment.
OpenStreetMap / Nominatim — map display and address geocoding. No personal data sent.
n8n (self-hosted) — automation workflows on our own VPS. Data stays on our server.
We do not sell your data. Ever.
4. Data retention
Account data: retained while your account is active + 3 years after deletion
Payment records: 7 years (GST / income tax compliance)
Server logs: 30 days (no personal data in logs)
Location data from rides: deleted 30 days after job completion
5. Your rights (DPDP Act 2023)
Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023:
• Access: Request a copy of your personal data
• Correction: Update incorrect information
• Deletion: Request deletion of your account and all data
• Grievance: Raise a complaint with our Data Protection Officer

To exercise any right, contact: privacy@mykkr.in
We respond within 30 days.
6. Data security
• All data in transit: encrypted via HTTPS (TLS 1.2+)
• Phone numbers: encrypted at rest in database (AES-256)
• Servers: hosted on Hostinger VPS (India region), behind Cloudflare WAF
• Access: SSH key only, no password login, Fail2Ban active
• Payments: handled entirely by Razorpay — we never store card/UPI details
• Data breach: we will notify affected users and CERT-In within 6 hours of discovery
7. Cookies
My KKR does not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics scripts.
We use browser localStorage only to remember your admin pricing preferences (non-personal, stays in your browser).
No cookie consent banner is required for this use.
